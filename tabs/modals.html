📊 How Scoring Works

Total 100 points across all categories. Here's what each thing is worth:

🏋️ Gym Session — 20 pts
Full session: 20 pts · Half session: 10 pts · Rest day: 0 pts · Skipped (should've gone): −5 pts penalty
🏠 Home Exercise — 10 pts
Full session: 10 pts · Light / Short: 5 pts · None: 0 pts (no penalty)
🍽️ Food — 12 pts
Homemade: 12 pts · Healthy out: 8 pts · Junk food: 2 pts
🔥 Calories + 💪 Protein — 10 pts
Calories near 1500 target: up to 5 pts · Protein near 176g target: up to 5 pts
📚 Study — 28 pts total
Uni study: up to 12 pts (full at 2h) · Car courses: up to 8 pts (full at 1h) · Self dev: up to 8 pts (full at 1h)
💊 Pills + ⚖️ Weight — 10 pts
Pills taken: 8 pts · Weight logged: 2 pts
✨ Skincare — 6 pts
Full routine: 6 pts · Half done: 3 pts · Skipped: 0 pts
🧠 Mood + Pain — 11 pts
Mood (1–10): up to 6 pts · Low pain score: up to 5 pts
🤝 Social + 💧 No Soda + 🍎 Fruit/Veg — 16 pts
Met IRL: 8 pts · Chatted: 4 pts · Solo: 0 pts · No soda: 4 pts · Fruit/veg: 4 pts
🪥 Teeth + 🧘 Meditation — 8 pts
Brushed twice: 4 pts · Once: 2 pts · Meditated: 4 pts
😴 Sleep — 6 pts
Sleeping before midnight + waking 7–9am = up to 6 pts
